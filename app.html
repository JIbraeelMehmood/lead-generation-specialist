📖 How to Get Website Clients

1. Target the right businesses

The best prospects are established local service businesses with no website or a bad one: plumbers, roofers, electricians, dentists, salons, auto repair shops, cleaners, landscapers, and restaurants. They need steady calls, and each new customer is worth a lot to them. Look for 20+ reviews and 4+ rating — those owners are busy, have money, but lose customers who search online.

2. Sell a "digital identity", not just a website

Owners buy outcomes: being found on Google, looking trustworthy, getting bookings. Use the Digital Identity check to show their score and what's missing. Offer three packages (Starter, Growth, Premium). Most choose the middle one, and a monthly care plan gives you recurring income.

3. Show, don't tell

Build a quick demo homepage using their name, photos and reviews, then send them the link. Owners reply far more often to "I made this for you, take a look" than to a generic offer.

4. Channel by region

  • USA/UK: Phone calls and email. Call Tue–Thu, mid-morning. Follow up by email with the demo link.
  • Pakistan / India / UAE / Middle East: WhatsApp gets the most replies. Send a short voice note + demo link. In-person visits also work very well.
  • Everywhere: Instagram and Facebook DMs work for salons, cafés, bakeries, and boutiques.

5. Follow up — most deals happen here

Contact day 1 → follow up day 3 → day 7 → day 14. Keep it short and friendly each time.

Message templates

Cold email (USA/UK)

Subject: Quick idea for {Business} Hi {Name}, I found {Business} on Google. {Reviews} reviews at {Rating} stars is impressive — but I noticed you don't have a website, so people searching "{type} in {city}" land on your competitors instead. I put together a free sample homepage: {demo link} If you like it, I can have it live within a week for {price}. Worth a quick 10-minute call? {Your name} Reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

WhatsApp message

Hi {Name}, I'm {Your name}, a web designer. I saw {Business} has great reviews on Google but no website. I made a free sample site: {demo link}. Can I show you how it would bring more customers? 😊

Phone opener

"Hi, is this the owner? I'll be quick. I'm a web designer. I was searching for {type} in {city} and your reviews stood out, but I couldn't find a website. I made a free sample. Can I send it to you on WhatsApp or email?"

📋 How to get a Google Places API key
  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and create a project.
  2. Enable billing (a card is required; there is a free usage allowance — check current pricing).
  3. Open "APIs & Services → Library" → enable Places API (New).
  4. Open "Credentials" → Create API key → restrict to Places API (New).
  5. Paste the key above. It stays on your computer.

Set a daily quota limit in Google Cloud to avoid unexpected charges.
